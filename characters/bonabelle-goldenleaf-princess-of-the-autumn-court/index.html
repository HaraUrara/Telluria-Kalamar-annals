Bonabelle Goldenleaf, Princess of the Autumn Court

Player Character

She’s smart, charismatic, capricious, and used to getting her way. While she hasn’t exactly had the most hands-on of parenting, she got by with the help of her friends, and of course, her favorite gran-gran, Queen Titania.

The Question she’s been asked the most times: “Who let you in here?”

Has an insatiable sweet tooth.
