Pietra Malander, Artificer

Player Character

Pietra Malander is a slight girl with a long face, gifted with the features most prominent in Renaria: the sharp, aquiline nose; the dark brown hair, and olive skin. Dressed in the robes of a mage acolyte, she is never without her various tomes, always poring over them whenever there is a lull.
