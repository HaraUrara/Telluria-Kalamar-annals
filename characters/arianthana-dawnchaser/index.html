Born under the New moon, Arianthana received her first accolade at birth. More were soon to follow as she was blessed by the gods. Her parents called her Moonshadow for her auspicious birth, and with the hopes that she would grow up well.
Her village elders called her Dawnchaser for how she would play in the light just as the sun would rise, chasing the line between night and day. She could never catch the dark, but she would continue to run until her body gave out on her.

Called by justice to right the wrongs of the lands, Arianthana tried for many years to convince the elves of her nation that justice must come first. The great elven host is a nation of free spirits, valuing their freedom over the shackles of rules and regulations. She would constantly preach that Justice is all that a society needs. Very few of the elves would even listen to her, but those that did were usually the elderly.

She learned that Elven wisdom starts with justice, but that elves value more than Justice. Put off by her insistence that Justice is the only thing you need, they encouraged her to journey into foreign lands to broaden her horizons. She left the elven lands to spread the wisdom about Justice being the proper course in all things.

During her first venture into human lands, she came upon many unjust practices and laws that did not exist in her lands. She didn’t understand the culture or the language, so she retreated back to the woods. She lived alone in a nearby forest and trained until she could understand the humans beyond simple merchant tongue.

During that time of learning, she found that many injustices come from misunderstandings especially between the “good” and “evil” races. Arianthana is trying to learn the languages of the “evil” races so that such injustices could be avoided. This pursuit has served her and her companions well, having gotten them out of a few dangerous situations without creating bloodshed or injustice from either side.

She ventured forth after learning Pekalese to various wilderness towns. Her accomplishments from that journey amount to little more than enhancing the general reputation of paladins across the land. A few accomplishments that did amount to more left an indelible mark on her in the form of accolades from those local peoples.

Arianthana was awarded the title of Wolfsbane from a village who mainly deal with sheep. She developed a wall system that keeps the town safe from both wolf and warg attacks, and is simple enough to only need timber and labor. By thwarting the threat of wolves attacking the sheep while in town, and giving the village a safe place to retreat to during times of crisis, the local ruler bestowed this accolade upon Arianthana so that all will remember her contribution, and he currently uses the technique to fortify several towns within his domain.

During her travels, Arianthana chanced upon a village right before a solar eclipse. A doom-saying intellectual was predicting disaster upon the village, and threatening aloud the entire populace with his dark magics unless they would submit to his rule. While he sounded crazy to both Arianthana and the villagers, Arianthana stayed in the village as a precaution since his threat was to be carried out within the week. Sure enough, the next day, the intellectual summoned up paltry magics with great theatrics while a solar eclipse was occurring. The villagers were completely taken in by his act, but Arianthana was not fooled. She vowed to save the villagers from this charlatan, and engaged in single combat with the wizard. Not expecting such skilled resistance, he was subdued quickly, but continued to insist that the sun would not return until he was freed and worshiped. Thinking quickly, Arianthana told the villagers there was another way to undo the foul magics employed by the wizard: the building of an altar to the god of the sun and harvest. Knowing the villagers wouldn’t be smart enough to comprehend what a solar eclipse actually was, she convinced the villagers to work quickly throughout the eclipse period on the altar so that they were finishing the crude altar just before the moon would begin to move away from the sun. Upon completion, there were only a few tense seconds before the first villager rejoiced at the sun re-appearing on the other side of the eclipse. They called her Darksbane, not only for her banishing the darkness of the sun, but also for defeating Agustus “Darkspear”, the wizard who had originated the plot. She refused the compensation offered by the town because accepting would have been simply replacing Agustus in his plot instead of correcting that wrong.

While in Oakenhurst, she met up with a group of adventurers after the Sunless Citadell. She ventured hence with them to tackle a coven of vampires. The group was able to deal with most of the vampires, but not without losses. Arianthana and her group had their life force drained during the fight, leaving them weak enough to be unable to resist Belak the Outcast, who used the coven for protection while working on his foul plots. Her allies were sacrificed to his terrible altar quickly, but Arianthana was able to convince him to spare her life for a time in order to make Belak’s sacrifice more effective. It was Arianthana’s idea to sacrifice an innocent and then herself to the demon. This persuasion put the little human girl Mela into danger. Arianthana’s personal mission to see that the bugbear see the business end of her sword before he gets the chance to sacrifice Mela was aided by a group who would quickly become her friends.

After dealing with Belak, she found that there was an immediate need for her steady hand and calm mind. Solving the problem of a permanent place for the goblin tribe to live where it would be accepted and useful is one of her new personal missions in the pursuit of a Just world.

She always prays for the wisdom to find the perfect justice for every injustice in the world, and the strength to deliver that justice to all the worlds inhabitants.
